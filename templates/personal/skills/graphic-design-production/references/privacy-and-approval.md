# Context privacy and external approval

## Context packet

Before concept work, write a short packet containing only:

- the current brief and acceptance criteria;
- source IDs, provenance, and freshness for explicitly allowed references;
- dimensions, language, accessibility requirements, and output constraints;
- safe asset IDs and local paths needed for this run.

Exclude private messages and email, memory contents, credentials, account identifiers, unrelated
files, hidden metadata, and full transcripts. If a source is not needed to make or critique the
artifact, do not copy it into the packet. Sanitize names and IDs when they are not required for
the deliverable. Pass role cards the smallest packet slice they need. The manifest carries only
safe IDs, counts, statuses, and hashes.

## Dynamic roles

Role cards are temporary run labels, not persistent agents. A card names its input, one output,
and a stop condition:

- brief owner: acceptance criteria and stop conditions;
- source curator: safe source index with provenance;
- concept/layout lead: visual thesis and deterministic layout spec;
- Image2 image maker: safe imagery request and asset hash;
- independent critic: fresh-context findings and verdict;
- export verifier: manifest, hashes, and unverified gaps.

No card may access private messages or memory by default, create another permanent role, or
publish. If independent execution is unavailable, perform a separately framed critic pass in
the same task and mark the limitation honestly; never claim independence from identical
self-justification.

## Exact publication boundary

Local generation, critique, correction, and export do not authorize external action. Before any
upload, post, send, ad launch, account change, or publication, present one exact approval envelope:

```text
Account:
Site or channel:
Destination and audience:
Action:
Complete content or diff:
Attachments (exact files and hashes):
Visibility:
Material side effects:
```

An immediate affirmative answer applies once to that exact envelope and its mechanical final
submission step. Do not broaden it to a different destination, account, audience, attachment,
visibility, permission scope, or content. Verify the destination after execution and report an
uncertain result as unverified; do not retry automatically.
