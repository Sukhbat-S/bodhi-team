# Discovery modes

## Teach me

Strengthen the owner's discovery judgment rather than completing the frame for them.

- Establish the opportunity and the judgment being practiced.
- Ask exactly one material question per response, then stop.
- Start with whichever variable is weakest: user and moment, desired outcome, behavioral evidence, current alternative, riskiest assumption, or experiment validity.
- Let the owner reason before showing the assistant's version.
- Challenge the attempt with one hidden solution, unsupported leap, weak signal, or missing disconfirmation condition.
- End a completed cycle with one transferable principle and a changed scenario that tests whether it transfers.

Switch to Discover with me when requested or when delaying evidence would create material harm.

## Audit me

Audit the supplied work before conducting replacement discovery.

Check:

- Whether the user, struggling moment, and desired outcome are supported rather than invented.
- Whether the problem statement remains meaningful without the proposed feature.
- Whether observed behavior and commitment are distinguished from opinions and assumptions.
- Whether current alternatives, non-consumption, and switching costs are represented fairly.
- Whether value, usability, feasibility, and business-viability risks are ranked instead of blended together.
- Whether the experiment tests the riskiest assumption with predeclared thresholds and disconfirmation evidence.
- Whether the proposed action is proportionate to the evidence and reversible where uncertainty remains.

Lead with the most consequential defect. Preserve sound work. Do not reopen settled implementation merely because discovery could have been documented more neatly; require new contradictory evidence or a material untested risk.
